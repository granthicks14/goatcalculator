GOAT Calculator

A weighted equation ranks the greatest players ever - and shows exactly what each one needs to climb higher.

Every player from every sport, scored with the exact same equation, pooled together and ranked by total GOAT Score. Cross-sport comparisons are illustrative - each component is originally anchored to greatness within that player's own sport.

Component ratings are anchored to real career achievements (titles, awards, records), but the 0-100 values and weights are an original, editable formula — not an official statistic. Edit WEIGHTS or SPORTS in this file's source to reflect your own view of "greatest ever."
